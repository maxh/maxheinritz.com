A few years ago I started growing broccoli sprouts. I freeze them and put them into smoothies. The reason is two-fold: longevity/health and the joy of "gardening."

I heard about broccoli sprouts during COVID, when I, like many people, filled time by dialing up my consumption of longevity podcasts. Rhonda Patrick has for over a decade now extolled the virtues of [sulforaphane](https://www.foundmyfitness.com/topics/sulforaphane), a powerful compound present in sprouts.

Growing sprouts is easy, cheap, and satisfying. They are visually pleasing but their taste and smell is a bit... strong. I put just a little bit into my smoothies, perhaps not as much as I could or should be consuming. But better than nothing.

I follow the recipe that came with the sprouting jars:

- Place fresh sprouting seeds in the jars (1.5 tablespoons in each jar)
- Soak in clean water for 6-8 hours, then drain completely
- Rinse and strain twice a day
- Be sure to empty the water from the drip tray as it gets smelly
- Takes about four days. For the first two days, I keep them near my sink. For the last two days, I move them closer to the window.

Growing food is surprisingly fun, and I hope to do more of it in the future. It taps into some primal urge for ownership, independence, and efficiency.
