I eat the same salad almost every day -- at the office, at home, when traveling. I'm so habituated to it that I feel a brain fog withdrawal when I go too long without it. Over the years, I've distilled the recipe to its essence: simple, delicious, and versatile enough to pair with anything.

## The Core Four

My non-negotiable base ingredients:

- **Leafy greens**: Spring mix, 50/50 spring mix and spinach, or baby kale. Whole Foods brand edges out ALDI for texture and substance.

- **Chopped apple**: For years, I ate only Gala apples. But I'm a bit more flexible these days. Cosmic Crisp is good too. The refrigerated crunch, sweetness, and cool burst they provide is essential. The apples also provide a good anchor for the fork, to really secure each bite before eating.

- **Salted sunflower seeds**: The unsung hero. Unlike larger nuts, these tiny seeds cling to individual leaves after mixing, ensuring each bite delivers that satisfying medley of textures. They're oddly elusive when traveling, lurking in unpredictable grocery store corners.

- **Extra virgin olive oil**: A generous pour is all the dressing needed. ALDI's Bono glass bottles offer the best value, but I enjoy experimenting. Pro tip: a premium bottle of olive oil makes an excellent gift for anyone (including me) ... "something I wouldn't buy myself but thoroughly enjoy."

## Weekend Upgrades

When I'm feeling fancy or entertaining:

- Blueberries
- Goat cheese
- A splash of balsamic vinegar, fresh lemon juice, or a proper vinaigrette

## The Method

I go through two to three pound-boxes of greens and five to seven apples weekly. Everything gets thoroughly mixed in a large metal bowl — a practice I insist upon. Growing up, my parents never pre-mixed salads (barbaric, looking back). Nothing compares to a well-dressed, properly combined salad with consistent flavor in every bite. Yum!
